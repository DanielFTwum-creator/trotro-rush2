Privacy Policy for Trotro Rush

Last updated: 26 September 2026 · Document ID: TUC-ICT-PRIV-2026-001

1. Overview & Commitment

Trotro Rush is developed and published by Techbridge University College (TUC), Oyibi, Greater Accra, Ghana. We believe in privacy by design. Trotro Rush does not collect, record, process, transmit, or monetize any personal data from our players.

2. Data Collection and Processing

The application operates entirely offline on your device:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • No Advertising or Tracking: There are no third-party advertisements, behavioral trackers, or analytics SDKs embedded in the app.
  • No Cloud Syncing: Gameplay progress (unlocked stations, star ratings, and moves) is stored exclusively on your device's local database storage.

3. Web Server Access Logs

When downloading updates or visiting our website (https://ai-tools.techbridge.edu.gh/trotro-rush/), standard web server access logs (IP address, user agent, timestamp) are temporarily recorded under TUC's institutional security policy for operational integrity and fraud prevention. These logs are never correlated with gameplay activity.

4. User Rights and Data Erasure

Under the Ghana Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843) and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you retain full rights over your data. Because all game progress is stored locally on your device, you can delete all data at any moment by tapping Reset All Progress in the in-game Settings menu, or by clearing the application storage via your device operating system.
